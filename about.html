About My Webpage

Hi, I'm Bryan — a full-stack developer with a soft spot for testing things until they break (on purpose). My background spans Java and Python, with hands-on work across Spring Boot, Flask, and React, and I genuinely enjoy the process of taking a feature from a rough idea to something tested, working, and shipped.

I got my start through Revature's training program, building full-stack applications and learning to treat quality as part of the build — not an afterthought. These days I'm expanding that foundation through Harvard's CS50, and this site is part of that journey: the page you're on, the project tracker, and the login system behind it were all built as I learned.

On the Projects page, you can find details about my recent work and contributions, accompanied by descriptions and links to each project.

Feel free to contact me through the Contact page.
